# Watchlist: what the monthly review re-reads

Fetch with WebSearch / web-fetch, or `founder-cto/polite_fetch.py` (respects robots.txt). wetten.overheid.nl refuses automated fetches: use officielebekendmakingen.nl or ask the founder for the text.

## Netherlands
- Belastingdienst tables (voorlopige aanslag, loonheffingen "nieuw in 2027"): belastingdienst.nl
- KVK belastingtarieven page for the year: kvk.nl/geldzaken/belastingtarieven-YYYY/
- Belastingplan status: rijksoverheid.nl (Prinsjesdag stukken), eerstekamer.nl (vote), Staatsblad on officielebekendmakingen.nl
- BTW rate changes and e-invoicing: belastingdienst.nl BTW pages

## Mexico
- DOF (dof.gob.mx): reforms to LISR, LIVA, CFF; Ley de Ingresos; RMF and its Anexo 8 (tariffs)
- SAT (sat.gob.mx): RESICO, platforms, retentions; INEGI for UMA (January)
- Cámara de Diputados / Senado: Paquete Económico votes

## Spain
- AEAT (sede.agenciatributaria.gob.es): IRPF manual, IS, IVA; BOE for Presupuestos and reales decretos-ley
- Seguridad Social: RETA tramos each January
- ATIB / Govern de les Illes Balears: regional IRPF scale

## United Arab Emirates
- FTA (tax.gov.ae): corporate tax decisions, Small Business Relief, free zones, VAT
- MoF (mof.gov.ae): new laws and treaties

## Treaty
- officielebekendmakingen.nl (Tractatenblad) and SRE treaty site (cja.sre.gob.mx) for protocols; OECD MLI positions

## Calendar (when changes usually land)
| when | NL | MX |
| --- | --- | --- |
| January | new tables apply; Belastingdienst updates | UMA published (INEGI); RMF in force |
| Mid September | Prinsjesdag: Belastingplan for next year | 8 Sep: Paquete Económico sent to Congress |
| October | Tweede Kamer debates | Diputados vote (~20 Oct), Senado (~31 Oct) |
| November-December | Eerste Kamer vote, Staatsblad | DOF publication; RMF + Anexo 8 late December |
