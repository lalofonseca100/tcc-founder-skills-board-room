# founder-legal watchlist

Cadence: monthly (first working day), plus the events below. Re-read every source in
`sources.md` older than 90 days and close the "Open checks" list.

| what | where | why | cadence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| NL laws in force, Staatsblad | https://www.officielebekendmakingen.nl/ (search Staatsblad: arbeidsrelaties, Waadi, Auteurswet, Burgerlijk Wetboek) | VBAR entry date, Zelfstandigenwet, Platform Work transposition | monthly |
| NL DBA enforcement | Belastingdienst arbeidsrelaties pages; Kamerbrieven SZW/Financiën | fines policy each January | monthly, and in December |
| EUR-Lex new acts | https://eur-lex.europa.eu/ (AI Act dates, Late Payment, Platform Work, GDPR changes) | staged dates, new rules | monthly |
| EP Legislative Observatory | 2023/0323(COD) Late Payment | stalled proposal | quarterly |
| Mexico DOF | https://www.dof.gob.mx/ (LFT, LFPDPPP reglamento, LFDA, Código de Comercio) | reforms often published in Dec-Jan | monthly |
| Mexico Diputados index | https://www.diputados.gob.mx/LeyesBiblio/ (last reform date per law) | check our law-index entries | quarterly |
| Spain BOE | https://www.boe.es/ (RD 607/2026 artists from 25 May 2027, Ley 3/2004, LPI, Crea y Crece e-invoicing order) | artist contracts, payment terms | monthly |
| Texas Legislature | https://capitol.texas.gov/ and statutes.capitol.texas.gov (sessions in odd years) | non-competes, AI (TRAIGA), privacy | quarterly; monthly during sessions |
| US: California legislature, FTC, NY | leginfo.legislature.ca.gov; ftc.gov; nysenate.gov | talent agencies, non-competes, contractor tests | quarterly |
| HCCH status tables | https://www.hcch.net/ (Choice of Court 2005, Judgments 2019) | new parties (US) | quarterly |
| UNCITRAL status | CISG, New York Convention | new parties/reservations | yearly |
| Sector codes | Fair Practice Code, NLdigital Voorwaarden, Common Paper, Bonterms | benchmark versions | yearly |
