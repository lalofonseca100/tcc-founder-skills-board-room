# Checklists per contract type

Clause ids refer to `clauses.md`. The `--type` names match `contract_review.py`.
"Ask" = facts to get from the founder or counterpart before drafting.

| type | use for | must have | ask |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `artist` | agency/booking agreement with an artist or performer (freelance) | C-01, C-02, C-03, C-07, C-08, C-09, C-11, C-12, C-13, C-18, C-20, C-21, C-23, C-24 | artist's legal form and VAT status; fee range; who insures the work; rights to images; travel and rider; how our fee is paid (by client or by artist) |
| `client` | booking/production terms with a festival, brand, venue, developer | C-02 to C-11, C-12 (licence of images), C-14, C-21, C-23, C-25 | event dates and site; permits and who holds them; budget and deposit; public liability; cancellation tolerance |
| `services` | MSA + SOW for software, AI, media or consulting work | C-02 to C-06, C-09, C-10, C-12, C-17, C-18, C-19, C-20, C-22, C-23, C-25 | deliverables and acceptance; data involved; AI tools used; hosting; who owns what; support period |
| `nda` | before sharing ideas, plans, client lists | C-17, C-06 (term), C-23 | mutual or one-way; what is confidential; how long |
| `supplier` | production, freight, rental, freelancers we hire | C-02 to C-06, C-08, C-09, C-11, C-17, C-18, C-22, C-23 | price basis; delivery and risk (Incoterms for goods); insurance; replacement if they fail |
| `management` | artist/DJ management agreement | C-01, C-02 (services and what the manager does *not* do), C-03 (commission base: gross or net, which income streams, post-term commission), C-06, C-15, C-17, C-18, C-20, C-21, C-23 | territories where gigs happen (Spain: artist employment regime, new RD 607/2026 from 25 May 2027; California/New York booking licences; Texas: no licence since 2011); commission %; expenses; term and sunset clause; who signs gig contracts |
| `partnership` | co-founders, joint venture, letter of intent | C-26, C-12, C-16, C-14, C-17, C-23 | split, vesting, cash vs. work, roles, time per week, exit |
| `dpa` | processor agreement under GDPR art. 28 / Mexican equivalent | C-18, C-17, C-06, C-09 | data categories; sub-processors; country of servers; breach contact |
| `sale` | selling or buying physical goods across borders | C-02, C-03, C-09, C-08, C-23 (CISG on purpose), Incoterms 2020 | goods, quantities, delivery place, risk of loss, warranty period |

## Universal pre-signing checklist

1. Right parties, right legal form, authority to sign (C-01).
2. Price, VAT and withholding confirmed with founder-tax (C-21).
3. Every number and date in the contract matches the proposal and the money plan.
4. Liability capped and mirrored; insurance matches the cap (C-09, C-11).
5. Exit exists for both sides (C-06, C-07, C-08).
6. IP and data clear (C-12, C-18, C-19).
7. Law and court chosen on purpose (C-23).
8. `contract_review.py` run, red flags answered, lawyer gates checked.
9. Signed copy stored (e-signature record; NOM-151 in Mexico for key contracts) and
   key dates (renewal, notice, deposit) in the calendar.
